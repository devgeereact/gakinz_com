About GAkinz
GAkinz is a web design and development studio that takes pride in work crafted with care, from the look of a site to how it behaves in a visitor's hands. We sit at the crossroads of design and development, so a client gets one team that both draws the vision and builds it properly. GAkinzOS is the system that runs the studio: it tracks every lead, client, project and pound, so the team can spend its hours on the craft instead of the admin.
Our Mission
GAkinz exists to give businesses one team for both design and build, so their website looks right and works right, without the handoff gaps that slow most projects down.
Our Vision
A web where any business can hand its whole site to one studio and watch it move from sketch to launch without ever falling between design and code.
What We Build
GAkinzOS is the studio operating system: leads, clients, projects, proposals, invoices, a client portal, time tracking and support, all under one roof and one brand.
