## Commercial priority

Service enquiries and client retention should pay for the studio first. Useful resources can build trust, attract search traffic and support secondary affiliate/ad revenue. Do not design around encouraging ad clicks. [Google's AdSense policies](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/48182) prohibit encouraging clicks and confusing ads with content.

The right niche is practical technology for small businesses, tied to the work GAkinz actually does. The owner confirmed six services on 10 October 2026: web design, web development, ecommerce, website care, general IT support and IT consultancy. Cover business websites, online shops, everyday IT, sensible technology choices and the equipment that helps people run those businesses. Networking and hardware repair are not services, so articles about routers or hardware are buying advice only and never imply GAkinz installs or repairs them.

Pilot six to eight complete useful articles over time, not mass publication. This is an editorial workload suggestion, **not an AdSense minimum**. Publish one substantial piece every two weeks if manageable. Update useful older pieces rather than writing near-duplicate buying guides. Use real screenshots/examples with permission, a named author, accurate published/updated dates and an honest distinction between first-hand tests and researched comparisons.

A recommendation explains who it suits, compatibility, limitations and an alternative. “I tested” requires actual testing. A tool is not recommended only because it pays a commission. No copied reviews, retailer descriptions or AI-generated product claims. Mark commercial relationships clearly before the relevant content.

## AdSense plan

[Google's site readiness guidance](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/7299563) calls for original relevant content and a good experience. Approval and earnings are not guaranteed. A portfolio plus a handful of thin articles is not a monetisation strategy.

1. Finish useful public content, navigation, About/contact and accurate privacy/cookie notices.
2. Verify owner control of the intended domain/account before any application or connection.
3. Have the owner apply or confirm existing site approval. Record status without storing account credentials.
4. Before serving relevant regional ads, implement Google's [certified CMP requirements for the EEA, UK and Switzerland](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/13554020), including the required TCF integration. A handmade cookie banner is not sufficient evidence of compliance.
5. Test consent accept, reject, settings change and withdrawal with network inspection. Review actual [ICO storage/access guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/what-are-storage-and-access-technologies/), especially advertising tags. Do not assume all analytics are exempt or that all analytics require the same consent treatment.
6. Pilot one labelled in-article slot and, if appropriate, one end-of-article slot. Reserve space to avoid layout movement. Initially exclude home, services, pricing, work, contact, login, proposals, legal pages and all private/client routes.
7. Check mobile reading, focus, performance and accidental-click risk. Do not place an ad where it resembles a menu, download or booking action.
8. Review revenue and impact after a useful period of genuine traffic, such as eight to twelve weeks. Disable the pilot if support load, performance or buyer distraction outweighs income.

Consent rejection must preserve access to the service content. Never ask clients, community members or friends to click ads, click ads yourself, buy artificial traffic or use incentivised visits. Keep third-party monetisation scripts out of authenticated layouts. Because public and private surfaces share an origin, review CSP/script boundaries and session exposure before adding scripts: avoiding private routes alone does not isolate localStorage from public-page scripts. Compare safer session/storage options in a focused auth review, including CSRF/CORS implications if cookies are proposed.

## Amazon Associates plan

The UK [Operating Agreement](https://affiliate-program.amazon.co.uk/help/operating/agreement) and [Programme Policies](https://affiliate-program.amazon.co.uk/help/operating/policies/) govern qualifying revenue and allowed uses. The account, marketplace and all intended website/social properties must be approved appropriately. Do not presume every private channel or app is an eligible placement.

Use Amazon's linking tools for a valid product URL and approved tracking ID. Begin with manually selected links in useful articles, with no catalogue import, price scraper, product-sync jobs or Amazon API integration. Do not hard-code prices, ratings or retailer images unless rights and current programme conditions permit the specific use; “Check current price on Amazon” avoids a stale price promise.

[Amazon's disclosure guidance](https://affiliate-program.amazon.co.uk/help/node/topic/GPXFHVYZMTGPUMPE) requires clear link disclosure and the statement: **“As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.”** Place the statement visibly on the site and a clear local disclosure beside/before relevant recommendation content. [ASA affiliate guidance](https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/affiliate-marketing.html) also requires commercial content to be recognisable. Use a prominent “Ad” label where applicable; a distant footer or obscure hashtag is not a substitute.

Proposed recommendation block in an existing article: product name, business use, suitability, limitation, approved URL and last manual review date. Prefer owner photographs or properly licensed assets. Mark outbound commercial links with appropriate `rel="sponsored"`; validate allowed URL schemes and intended destination. No hidden redirects or affiliate-tag alteration in the browser.

For social/community distribution, share the useful public guide first. Do not send direct affiliate links through WhatsApp, email or other placements until the owner checks the programme rules and the actual property/channel eligibility. No member incentives, guaranteed commission claims or suggesting associates are employed by Amazon.

## Measurement without another dashboard product

| Metric | Where to start | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Suitable service enquiries and won projects | Existing enquiry/project records | Core studio income |
| Published article visits/search engagement | Approved analytics/Search Console when authorised | Whether resources help the intended audience |
| Qualified affiliate clicks and actual commission | Amazon's reporting; modest page/link tracking only if lawful | Traffic is not the same as qualifying purchases |
| Ad revenue and RPM | AdSense reports | Evaluate secondary income against reader impact |
| Content effort and enquiries per topic | Short monthly owner review | Stop maintaining content that adds no value |

Illustrative arithmetic, not a forecast: 10,000 page views at an assumed £5 page RPM would yield £50; 300 affiliate clicks × assumed 4% purchase rate × £60 qualifying basket × assumed 3% commission would yield £21.60 before adjustments. These values are invented scenarios, not current rates or expected results. Real rates, eligible products, returns and traffic determine income. One useful company project may be worth much more than early advertising revenue.
